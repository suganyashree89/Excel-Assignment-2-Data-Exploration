📦 Product Details Database

📌 Project Description

The Product Details Database is a structured dataset containing information about different products, their brands, prices, available quantities, and categories.
This project is designed to organize product information and support inventory management, data analysis, and business intelligence.

📊 Dataset Overview

Total Records: 5
Total Columns: 8
Total Brands:  5
Product Categories: Electronics, Fashion, Kitchen, Unknown
Domain:             Retail & Product Inventory Management
📋 Dataset Columns

Column Name	Description
Product_ID	  Unique product identification number
Product_Name	Name of the product
Brand	Product brand or manufacturer
Original_Price	Original price of the product
Selling_Price	Selling price of the product
Quantity	Available product quantity
Category	Main product category
Sub_Category	Product subcategory


📈 Key Insights

The dataset contains 5 different products.
Electronics is the category with the highest number of products.
Dell, Nike, Keurig, Samsung, and North Face are the five brands.
Coffee Maker has the highest available stock quantity of 40 units.
Backpack has missing category information.
Original Price and Selling Price are identical for all products.
