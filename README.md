Product Details Database

📌 Project Overview
The Product Details Database is a structured dataset containing product information across multiple categories, including Electronics, Fashion, and Kitchen appliances.
This dataset helps organize and manage product details such as product ID, product name, brand, product price, selling price, quantity, and product category.

📊 Dataset Information

The database contains 5 product records with information about different products and their categories.


Dataset Columns

Column Name	      Description
Product_ID	      Unique identifier for each product
Product_Name	    Name of the product
Brand	Product     manufacturer or brand
Original_Price	  Original price of the product
Selling_Price	    Current selling price
Quantity	        Available product quantity
Category	        Main product category
Sub_Category	    Product subcategory


📁 Product Categories

The dataset includes the following product categories:

Electronics:    Laptop, Smartphone
Fashion:  Sneakers
Kitchen:  Coffee Maker
Unknown:  Backpack (category information requires verification)

🔍 Key Observations

Electronics contains the highest number of products.
Dell and Samsung are the brands represented in Electronics.
The Coffee Maker has the highest available quantity (40 units).
The Backpack record has missing category information.
Original prices and selling prices are identical across all five records.
